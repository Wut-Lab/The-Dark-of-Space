In the Dark of Space

Campaign

Choose your machine. Clear sector after sector, refit between them, and see how far you get before your armor gives out.

Timed Attack

Three minutes on the clock and endless waves. Score as much scrap as you can before time runs out.

  • Each hit costs 5 seconds instead of armor
  • Waves speed up every 30 seconds; no refit stops
  • Destroyed units can drop time capsules: fly over one to grab it

Unlocks

Nothing to unlock yet. New machines and refits earned across runs will appear here.

How to Play

The goal

Survive as many sectors as you can. Each sector is a wave of enemy units: destroy every one to clear it, choose a refit, and push on to the next. When your armor runs out, the run is over.

Topics

Controls

Keyboard

  • A / D or ← / →: strafe left and right
  • Shift: dash
  • Esc or P: pause

Touch

  • Drag sideways anywhere to strafe
  • Tap Dash (bottom right) to dash
  • Tap Pause (top right) to pause

Firing

  • Your guns fire on their own, straight up the field

Dash

  • A quick sideways burst in the direction you're moving
  • You can't be hurt during a dash
  • Each dash uses EN (the yellow bar), which refills over time

Your Machines

Kestrel

  • Mobile frame: 12 armor
  • Heavy rifle: slow fire, big damage
  • Slowest strafe

Gale

  • Interceptor: 6 armor
  • Rapid lasers: fast fire, light damage
  • Fastest strafe

Falcon

  • Strike fighter: 9 armor
  • Twin cannons: medium fire rate and damage
  • Medium strafe

Armor & Damage

The armor bar

  • Green: 76–100%
  • Yellow: 26–75%
  • Red: 25% and below. The bar pulses and a warning beep sounds

Taking hits

  • Enemy shots and diving units both cost armor
  • The screen edges flash red when you're hit
  • You get a brief moment of safety after each hit

When armor hits zero

  • Your machine is destroyed and the run ends

Sectors

Start

  • A SECTOR banner shows the sector number and how many units to destroy

During

  • Units fly in two at a time in sweeps, loops and figure-eights, then form up at the top
  • From the formation, units break off and dive at you
  • Units left in the top bar counts down as you destroy them

End

  • When Units left reaches zero, SECTOR CLEAR flashes
  • Then you choose a refit and pick your next stop on the route
  • Each sector brings more units, and they dive more often

The Route

After each refit, choose where to go next. You always get one Combat option, plus two others.

Stops

  • Combat: a normal sector
  • Elite (from sector 3): tougher units. Clear it for +15 scrap and 2 refits
  • Salvage Yard: spend scrap on refits, Tune-ups, armor repairs or a restock
  • Repair Dock: restores half your armor. Only offered when you're damaged
  • Unknown Signal: a random event. Could be salvage, a deal, or an ambush

Fixed sectors

  • Every 5th sector is a Capital Unit boss: shoot off its 3 turrets to expose the core. Worth +30 scrap and 2 refits
  • Every other 4th sector is a Bonus Stage
  • Every stop counts as a sector, so a yard or dock still moves you one step closer to the next boss

Enemies

Skirmisher

  • Tan, with arm blades
  • Doesn't shoot; dives at you to ram

Sniper

  • Grey, with a long rifle and a radar dish
  • Fires aimed shots, from the formation and while diving
  • Appears from sector 2

Heavy

  • Large and rust red, with shoulder cannons
  • Tough; fires a three-shot spread
  • Appears from sector 3, in the back row

Capital unit "Bastion"

  • A boss every 5th sector: a slab-hulled carrier with three bolted-on turrets
  • The core is armored until all three turrets are shot off
  • Turrets fire aimed shots; the core fires a five-shot fan, faster once exposed

Bonus Stages

  • Every 4th sector (4, 8, 12, 16…) is a Bonus Stage, except when a boss sector lands there (20, 40…)
  • 40 units sweep past in five groups of 8
  • They never shoot or ram you
  • Each one you destroy pays 3 scrap
  • Destroy all 40 for a PERFECT bonus of +20 scrap

Refits & Scrap

Refits

  • After each sector, choose one of three refits
  • Options include more damage, faster fire, extra armor, speed, extra projectiles, shots that pass through units, and repairs
  • Refits last for the rest of the run

Rarity

  • Common (grey), Uncommon, Rare and Contraband
  • Contraband refits are the strongest, but each one has a drawback
  • Elite and boss rewards roll rarer refits more often

Salvage Yard

  • Rarer refits cost more scrap, and prices rise each sector
  • Tune-up (10 scrap) is always in stock: a small boost to damage, fire rate, strafe speed and max armor. Buy it as often as you like

Scrap

  • Skirmisher: 1 · Sniper: 2 · Heavy: 5
  • Units destroyed mid-dive pay double
  • Scrap is your score for the run

Timed Attack

The clock

  • You start with 3:00, shown at the top of the screen
  • It flashes red under 30 seconds
  • When it reaches zero, the run ends and your scrap is your score

Hits

  • You have no armor bar in this mode: each hit costs 5 seconds

Waves

  • Waves keep coming with no refit stops or bonus stages
  • Every 30 seconds, units dive faster and more often

Time capsules

  • Destroyed units sometimes drop a glowing capsule. Fly over it to grab it
  • Green (common): +5 seconds
  • Gold (rare): +5 seconds and 2.5 seconds of invulnerability
  • Capsules drift toward you and vanish if you miss them

Power-ups

Destroyed units sometimes drop a glowing capsule with a dark band. Fly over it to grab it. Heavies and units shot mid-dive drop them more often. They work in every mode.

The three power-ups

  • EMP (blue): clears enemy fire and freezes every unit for 2.5 seconds
  • Barrier (violet): blocks the next 3 hits, then breaks
  • Spread Shot (orange): fires a 5-way fan for 5 seconds

Tips

  • Active power-ups show as rings under the top bar. The ring empties as time runs out
  • Grabbing one you already have adds time, or refills the Barrier
  • Capsules blink just before they slip past you

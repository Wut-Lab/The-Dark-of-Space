Campaign

Choose your machine. Clear sector after sector, upgrade between them, and see how far you get before your armor gives out.

Timed Attack

Three minutes on the clock and endless waves. Score as much scrap as you can before time runs out.

  • Each hit costs 5 seconds instead of armor
  • Waves speed up every 30 seconds; no upgrade stops
  • Destroyed units can drop time capsules: fly over one to grab it

Unlocks

Earn these by reaching goals across your runs. Unlocked upgrades join the Campaign pool.

How to Play

Destroy every enemy unit to clear a sector, pick an upgrade, and fly on. When your armor runs out, the run is over.

Controls

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys. On touch, drag anywhere (play in landscape).
  • Your guns fire on their own.
  • Dash with Shift or the Dash button. Each dash uses EN.
  • Pause with Esc, P or the Pause button.
  • On a gamepad, move with the left stick or D-pad, dash with A or the right bumper or trigger, and pause with Start. In menus, A selects and B goes back.

Campaign

  • After each sector, pick an upgrade and choose where to go next.
  • Every 5th sector is a boss.
  • Enemies drop scrap and sometimes power-ups.
  • Spend banked scrap in the Shop.
  • Play well to unlock new crafts, upgrades and paint jobs.

Timed Attack

  • Score as much scrap as you can before the clock runs out.
  • Each hit costs time, and time capsules add it back.
  • Some power-ups only appear in Timed Attack.

Daily Run

  • A Campaign run that changes each day. Everyone gets the same craft, routes and upgrade offers.
  • On Pilot's Choice days, fly any craft you own.
  • Start by picking 1 of 3 upgrades, and face the day's enemy twist.
  • Try as often as you like. Your best of the day is kept.

Hard Mode

  • Play Timed Attack with the lights out: you only see what's ahead of you.
  • Enemies are tougher, your starting loadout is random, and your score is bigger.

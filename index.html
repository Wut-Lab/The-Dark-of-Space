In the Dark of Space

Campaign

Choose your machine. Clear sector after sector, upgrade between them, and see how far you get before your armor gives out.

Timed Attack

Three minutes on the clock and endless waves. Score as much scrap as you can before time runs out.

  • Each hit costs 5 seconds instead of armor
  • Waves speed up every 30 seconds; no upgrade stops
  • Destroyed units can drop time capsules: fly over one to grab it

Shop

Total Scrap: 0

Unlocks

Earn these by reaching goals across your runs. Unlocked upgrades join the Campaign pool.

How to Play

The goal

Survive as many sectors as you can. Each sector is a wave of enemy units: destroy every one to clear it, choose an upgrade, and push on to the next. When your armor runs out, the run is over.

Topics

Controls

Keyboard

  • A / D or ← / →: strafe left and right
  • Shift: dash
  • Esc or P: pause
  • The game goes fullscreen when a run starts and leaves it when you return to the main menu

Touch

  • Phones and tablets play in landscape: turn your device sideways when a run starts. In portrait the game pauses until you do
  • Drag sideways anywhere to strafe
  • Tap Dash (bottom right) to dash
  • Tap Pause (top right) to pause

Firing

  • Your guns fire on their own, straight up the field

Dash

  • A quick sideways burst in the direction you're moving
  • You can't be hurt during a dash
  • Each dash uses EN (the yellow bar), which refills over time

Your Machines

Kestrel

  • Mobile frame: 12 armor
  • Heavy rifle: slow fire, big damage
  • Slowest strafe

Gale

  • Interceptor: 6 armor
  • Rapid lasers: fast fire, light damage
  • Fastest strafe

Falcon

  • Strike fighter: 9 armor
  • Twin cannons: medium fire rate and damage
  • Medium strafe

Swift (Shop, 500 Total Scrap)

  • Speed frame: 8 armor, torpedo head and torso, winged shoulders
  • Light rifle: same damage and fire rate as Falcon
  • Fastest strafe, and EN refills 30% faster for more dashes

Armor & Damage

The armor bar

  • Green: 76–100%
  • Yellow: 26–75%
  • Red: 25% and below. The bar pulses and a warning beep sounds

Taking hits

  • Enemy shots and diving units both cost armor
  • The screen edges flash red when you're hit
  • You get a brief moment of safety after each hit

When armor hits zero

  • Your machine is destroyed and the run ends

Sectors

Start

  • A SECTOR banner shows the sector number and how many units to destroy
  • You get about 5 seconds of open space before each wave flies in
  • Each sector has its own music: Starfall Run in normal sectors and Gold Rush in Bonus Stages. In boss sectors the music cuts out until the boss arrives, then Iron Titan kicks in. Some zones have their own sector and boss themes

During

  • Units fly in two at a time in sweeps, loops and figure-eights, then form up at the top
  • From the formation, units break off and dive at you
  • Units left in the top bar counts down as you destroy them

Waves

  • Each sector has 2 waves (3 from sector 10). The banner shows which wave you're on
  • Clear a wave and WAVE CLEAR flashes; the next wave arrives a moment later
  • Boss and Bonus sectors are a single wave

End

  • When the last wave's Units left reaches zero, SECTOR CLEAR flashes
  • Then you choose an upgrade and pick your next stop on the route
  • Each sector brings more units, and they dive more often
  • New enemy types join gradually: Heavies from sector 4, Mines from 7, Lancers from 11, Ooze from 21. Elite sectors may show one a little early

The Route

After each upgrade, choose how to reach the next sector. Every choice ends in a fight: stops happen on the way, then the sector's wave arrives.

Stops

  • Combat: straight into a normal sector (offered when there are few other choices)
  • Elite (from sector 3): tougher units. Clear it for +30 scrap and 2 upgrades
  • Salvage Yard: make one purchase (an upgrade, a Tune-up or an armor repair), then fight
  • Repair Dock: restores half your armor, then fight. Only offered when you're damaged
  • Unknown Signal: a random event, then fight. Could be salvage, a deal, or an ambush (the ambush is the fight)

Fixed sectors

  • Sectors are grouped into zones of ten. After each zone's second boss you reach a branch and choose which zone to fly into next. Different zones hold different dangers. Watch for a lane glowing red: something is about to come down it
  • Every 5th sector is a Capital Unit: a boss with its own attacks to learn. Worth +60 scrap and 2 upgrades
  • Sector 4.5: sometimes a Bonus Stage opens before the boss
  • You can't skip sectors: every step toward the boss is a fight
  • Save and Quit (pause menu) ends the session; pick Resume saved run under Campaign to restart that sector from its beginning

Enemies

Skirmisher

  • Tan, with arm blades
  • Doesn't shoot; dives at you to ram

Sniper

  • Grey, with a long rifle and a radar dish
  • Fires aimed shots, from the formation and while diving
  • Appears from sector 2

Heavy

  • Large and rust red, with shoulder cannons
  • Tough; fires a three-shot spread
  • Appears from sector 4, in the back row

Mine

  • A dark spiked sphere with a rust band
  • Drifts in and parks just ahead of your row, blocking that lane and soaking up your shots
  • Tough. Touching it hurts; dash through or destroy it
  • After about 15 seconds it lifts off and leaves on its own
  • Appears from sector 7

Mote

  • A small flickering ice crystal drone, found in some zones
  • Never attacks, and one hit destroys it, but it flits around in front of you and soaks up shots meant for others

Praetorian

  • A Roman guard mech in steel and gold: sideways red crest, short sword, shield and a purple cape. Found in Legion Space
  • Paces side to side in the upper field behind its shield
  • Its chest cannon glows orange, then fires an aimed shot twice as wide as normal, every few seconds
  • Tougher than most units

Splinter

  • A cluster of red crystal on a chunk of rock, found in some zones
  • Hovers near the top, firing single shards at you
  • When destroyed it bursts into three shards flung your way, so don't kill it point-blank

Lancer

  • A dark red dart-shaped drone
  • Hovers for a moment, slides to the left or right edge, flashes, then rams where you were. Fast, but fragile
  • Appears from sector 11

Ooze

  • A big green slime blob. Never shoots
  • Creeps above your row, then squashes flat and flashes before it lunges at you
  • Leaves a glowing goo trail, with a big splat where it lands. Goo slows you
  • Each kill splits it into two smaller, faster copies: 7 blobs in all
  • Appears from sector 21

Capital units

  • Every zone ends in a boss: a Capital Unit, far bigger and tougher than anything before it
  • Each one fights its own way. Watch for its tells (flashes, shudders, red warnings) and learn its patterns
  • Bring it down for +60 scrap and 2 upgrades

Bonus Stages

  • Sometimes a Bonus Stage opens as sector 4.5, right before a boss
  • 40 units sweep past in five groups of 8
  • They never shoot or ram you, and one hit destroys any of them
  • Some groups cross the field; others loop back out the side they came in, so move to meet them
  • Each one you destroy pays 3 scrap
  • Destroy all 40 for a PERFECT bonus of +20 scrap

Upgrades & Scrap

Upgrades are permanent for the rest of the run. Power-ups are different: temporary boosts that enemies drop and you fly over to use.

Getting upgrades

  • After each sector, choose one of three
  • Buy them at Salvage Yards
  • Some Unknown Signals pay out in upgrades
  • Each upgrade has 3 tiers (I, II, III): taking it again stacks the effect. At tier III it leaves the pool for the rest of the run
  • Pause and tap Upgrades to see everything you've collected

Current upgrades

  • Tune-up (Common): +5% damage, fire rate and strafe speed, +1 max armor
  • Salvage claws (Uncommon): +1 scrap per unit destroyed
  • Rail barrel (Rare): shots fly 50% faster and pass through 1 more unit
  • Hair trigger (Contraband): +1 projectile per volley, but strafe 25% slower
  • Enhanced EMPs (Common): EMP power-ups freeze units 0.2 s longer per tier (2.7 / 2.9 / 3.1 s)
  • Beam Shot (Uncommon): shots become beams with a long tail that also deals damage. +1 damage, but fire 30% slower; the slowdown drops to 20% at tier II and 10% at tier III
  • Overcharged Cells (Common): energy recharges 20% faster per tier
  • Ricochet Shield (Uncommon): dashing raises a shield that bounces enemy shots off at an angle set by your motion (2 damage to units they hit); dashes cost 15 more energy. Tier II: the shield deals 4 contact damage. Tier III: no extra energy cost
  • Venom Launcher (Rare): every 10th volley fires a green grenade: +2 damage to the unit it hits and 2 to units nearby. Tier II: +1 center damage. Tier III: +1 blast damage

Unlockable upgrades

  • Some upgrades are earned on the main menu's Unlocks screen by reaching goals across runs
  • Bootleg Shields (Contraband): every 5th hit has a 50% chance to be blocked, and the next shot does +2 damage. Tier II: every 4th hit, 75%, +3. Tier III: every 3rd hit, always, +4. Each block halves your strafe speed for 0.4 s. Unlock: block 30 hits with Barrier power-ups

Shop upgrades

  • Buy these in the main-menu Shop with Total Scrap to add them to the pool
  • Ram Plating (Common, 100): collisions with units cost 1 less armor (never below 1), and the unit takes +3 damage per tier
  • Confetti Payload (Common, 80): destroyed units burst into confetti, with a 10% chance per tier to shake loose +2 scrap
  • Wide Shot (Common, 120): shots become 15% wider
  • Whip Shot (Uncommon, 240): every third volley, one green shot arcs across the screen for +1 damage
  • Tracking Shot (Rare, 400): every fifth volley, one blue shot homes in on the farthest unit for +1 damage

Rarity

  • Common (grey), Uncommon, Rare and Contraband
  • Contraband upgrades are the strongest, but each one has a drawback
  • Elite and boss rewards roll rarer upgrades more often

Salvage Yard

  • Rarer upgrades cost more scrap, and prices rise each sector
  • Tune-up (20 scrap) is always in stock: a small boost to damage, fire rate, strafe speed and max armor. One purchase per Salvage Yard visit, whatever you buy

Scrap

  • Skirmisher: 1 · Sniper: 2 · Heavy: 5
  • Units destroyed mid-dive pay double
  • Kills pay about 10% less than these values on average
  • Scrap is your score for the run
  • When a run ends (or you quit), half your scrap is banked as Total Scrap, shown on the main menu. The other half repairs your craft back to 100%

Timed Attack

The clock

  • You start with 3:00, shown at the top of the screen
  • It flashes red under 30 seconds
  • When it reaches zero, the run ends and your scrap is your score

Hits

  • You have no armor bar in this mode: each hit costs 5 seconds

Waves

  • Waves keep coming with no upgrade stops or bonus stages
  • Every 30 seconds, units dive faster and more often
  • New enemy types join quickly: Heavies from wave 2, Mines from 3, Lancers from 4, Ooze from 7

Time capsules

  • Destroyed units sometimes drop a glowing capsule. Fly over it to grab it
  • Green (common): +5 seconds
  • Gold (rare): +5 seconds and 2.5 seconds of invulnerability
  • Capsules drift toward you and vanish if you miss them

Power-ups

Power-ups are temporary boosts: destroyed units sometimes drop a glowing capsule with a dark band. Fly over it to grab it. Heavies and units shot mid-dive drop them more often. They work in every mode.

The three power-ups

  • EMP (blue): clears enemy fire and freezes every unit for 2.5 seconds
  • Barrier (violet): blocks the next 3 hits, then breaks
  • Spread Shot (orange): fires a 5-way fan for 5 seconds

Shop power-ups

  • Buy these in the main-menu Shop to add them to the drops
  • Patch Kit (silver, Common, 80): repairs 2 armor in Campaign, or adds 3 seconds in Timed Attack. Common power-ups drop twice as often as the others

Tips

  • Active power-ups show as rings under the top bar. The ring empties as time runs out
  • Grabbing one you already have adds time, or refills the Barrier
  • Capsules blink just before they slip past you

HOW TO PLAY

  • A/D or arrows strafe. On touch, play in landscape and drag sideways.
  • Your guns fire on their own, straight up the field.
  • Shift or the Dash button bursts sideways and makes you briefly invulnerable. It uses EN, which refills.
  • Units dive at you. Shoot them mid-dive for double scrap.
  • Clear a sector to pick one upgrade (Common, Uncommon, Rare or Contraband; Contraband has a drawback), then choose your next stop: Combat, Elite, Salvage Yard, Repair Dock or Unknown Signal. Every 5th sector is a boss. When armor hits zero, the run ends.
  • Save and Quit keeps the run: resume it from Campaign, back at the start of the current sector.
  • Each sector has 2 waves (3 from sector 10). A banner marks each wave: destroy every unit to clear it. Units left in the top bar counts down to zero.
  • At zero, SECTOR CLEAR flashes and you pick an upgrade before the next sector.
  • Every 5th sector is a boss. Every 10 sectors you choose which zone to fly into next. Sometimes sector 4.5 is a Bonus Stage: units loop past without shooting. Each kill pays 3 scrap; get all 40 for a +20 Perfect bonus.
  • Power-ups are temporary drops you fly over; upgrades are permanent for the run (see them under Upgrades in this menu). Blue EMP freezes units for 2.5 s, violet Barrier blocks 3 hits, orange Spread Shot fires a 5-way fan for 5 s.
  • Timed Attack: each hit costs 5 seconds. Grab green capsules for +5 s, gold for +5 s and brief invulnerability.
  • Esc or P pauses.

In the Dark of Space

You're a mercenary pilot taking contracts in a contested sector. Pick your machine, hold the line against each wave of mass-production units as they swoop in from formation, and refit between sorties. If your machine goes down, the run is over.

A/D or arrows to strafe, Shift to dash (uses EN, brief invulnerability). On touch, drag sideways to strafe and press Dash. Guns fire automatically. Units shot mid-dive drop double scrap.

HOW TO PLAY

  • A/D or arrows strafe. On touch, drag sideways.
  • Your guns fire on their own, straight up the field.
  • Shift or the Dash button bursts sideways and makes you briefly invulnerable. It uses EN, which refills.
  • Units dive at you. Shoot them mid-dive for double scrap.
  • Clear a sector to pick one refit. When armor hits zero, the run ends.
  • Each sector is one wave. A banner marks its start: destroy every unit to clear it. Units left in the top bar counts down to zero.
  • At zero, SECTOR CLEAR flashes and you pick a refit before the next sector.
  • Every 4th sector is a Bonus Stage: units loop past without shooting. Each kill pays 3 scrap; get all 40 for a +20 Perfect bonus.
  • Esc or P pauses.

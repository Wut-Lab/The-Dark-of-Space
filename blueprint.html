Capital units that end a zone. Easy bosses guard the early sectors.

Easy 3

Bastion Open Zone: 5, 10+60 scrap

Slab-hulled carrier with three bolted-on turrets. The core is armored until the turrets are shot off; the core fires a five-shot fan.

Warden Open Zone: 5, 10+60 scrap

Giant mech with an arm beam cannon. Laser-sight tell, then an aimed beam every few seconds; flashes half the field red, then sweeps a beam from that edge to the centre. Heavily armored; calls in skirmishers.

Minotaur Open Zone: 5, 10+60 scrap

Oversized Heavy with bull horns, twin shoulder cannons and a black inverted triangle on its forehead. Cannons fire aimed shots left, right, left at a steady beat. Every 5–8 s it stops, shudders and flashes a red lane at you, then rams down it for 3 damage.

Medium 4

The Caesar Legion Space: 15, 20+60 scrapLegion Space

Golden mech legionary: red iron crest on a T-visor helmet, round shield with red trim, spear. Bulky but rounded. Shoulder cannon over the shield fires 3-shot bursts that mildly track. Closes in, lowers its spear (red zone flashes) and sweeps it for 2 damage. Shudders, flashes a red lane and shield-rams for 3, then resets to the back middle.

The Glacier Frost Reach: 15, 20+60 scrapThe Frost Reach

Ice-bound juggernaut warship: dark hull, bridge tower, side sponsons, prow frost cannon, all encased in a jagged ice shell. Fires slow 5-shard fans; drops freeze fields on you (blue ring flashes, then halves your speed while inside, 4 s); calls in Rime Motes. At half health the shell shatters: faster fans, more fields.

Dry Ice Frost Reach: 15, 20+60 scrapThe Frost Reach

Frosty interceptor: wings fanning back in steps, a fin of ice, always wrapped in mist. Strafes back and forth firing straight rounds that loosely home in. Every 7–9 s mist rolls over your view; it repositions while you are blind, flashes a red lane, then fires a wide beam straight ahead (2 damage).

Onager Legion Space: 15, 20+60 scrapLegion Space

Large bronze-and-gold siege ship with a ram prow. Stage 1: holds the centre and lobs EMP bombs from the catapult on its back (blue ring marks the landing: 1 damage, speed halved for 1.5 s) while Lancers make their runs; the hull is armored until the catapult (separate target) is destroyed. Stage 2: rams like a Lancer (hover, red lane, charge for 3), then returns to the top.

How a run is laid out: areas (zones of five sectors) and the sector types inside them. Design notes for new areas go here.

Area layout 1

The Open Zone 5 sectorsSectors 1–10

The starting area: two bosses, at sectors 5 and 10, picked at random from Bastion, Warden and Minotaur. Sectors 1–4 are combat, sector 5 is a boss (then 6–9 and 10). Take 3 hits or fewer across the four combat sectors and a Bonus Stage opens as sector 4.5. Plain starfield, no hazards. Its name shows on each sector banner.

Sector types 7

Combat Sectors 1–4

Waves of Drudges (2 waves, 3 from sector 10) with a 5 s quiet start. Enemy health scales +8% per sector.

Elite Sector 3+

Tougher units (1.5× health). +30 scrap and 2 upgrades.

Salvage Yard Route

Shop for upgrades and repairs, then fight the sector.

Repair Dock Route

Restore half your armor, then fight the sector.

Unknown Signal Route

A random event, then the sector. Could be salvage, could be a trap.

Bonus Stage Sector 4.5

40 units in five groups of 8 that never shoot or ram; any hit destroys one. Gold Rush music.

Capital Unit Sector 5, 10, 15…

Boss set by the zone: The Open Zone picks Bastion, Warden or Minotaur; Legion Space is always The Caesar. +60 scrap and 2 upgrades.

Zones 2

Legion Space Branch: sectors 11–20Backdrop builtBosses: The Caesar or Onager

Cyberpunk meets the Roman Legion. Backdrop (built): stars over black space with sandy dust and haze; floating sandstone ruins with neon cyan and pink trim drift past on both sides: colonnades, arches, cyber towers with gold domes and, now and then, a Parthenon. Chosen at the branch after sector 10 (the only choice for now; two more zones to come). Bosses at 15 and 20 are picked from The Caesar and Onager. After sector 20 the branch repeats. Occasional obstacles drift through the field for the player to avoid (ideas: legion standard pylons, shield-wall barriers, debris from a cyber-colosseum). Obstacle built: Fallen Column, a lane glows red for 1 s, then a broken marble column tumbles down it every 6–10 s (1 damage on touch; 6 hp, shoot it apart for 2 scrap). Bosses: The Caesar or Onager. it already appears in the random boss pool until the area is built.

The Frost Reach Branch: sectors 11–20BuiltBosses: The Glacier or Dry Ice

Icy space wreckage. Backdrop: ice floes, icebergs and frozen warship hulks drift past below the field, snow dust blows sideways, an aurora ripples far ahead; space turns a deep blue-black; translucent white and pale blue frost veils and glassy ice crystals drift by. Bosses: The Glacier or Dry Ice. Obstacle: Ice Shard, a red lane flashes at an angle from a side edge, then a shard slides across it (1 damage; 4 hp, 2 scrap). Unit: Rime Mote, 3 per wave. Music: Whiteout Drift (sectors), Glacier Heart (boss).

Upgrades are permanent for the run, earned by buying, clearing sectors, objectives or events. Power-ups are temporary drops you fly over.

Odds per upgrade: Common 60%, Uncommon 27%, Rare 10%, Contraband 3%. Elite and boss rewards use 30 / 36 / 24 / 10. Shop price = base + sector number. Each upgrade has 3 tiers per run; at tier III it leaves the pool.

Pacing: one new threat at a time. Sectors 1–10: Skirmisher, Sniper (2), Heavy (4), Mine (7). 11–20: Lancer (11) plus the zone unit. 21–30: Ooze (21), a new hard enemy, plus the zone unit. 31–40: everything, plus the zone unit. Elites preview the next enemy a little early.

Hard 3

Void Bot Dark Space5 scrapHard

Slender dark grey and purple mech with violet eye slits, hard to see. Hovers in the upper field firing glowing purple 3-shot fans. Every 6–9 s it blinks (an unnatural zip) to one side just short of halfway down the lane, zips across to the other side, then dives at you, homing loosely, and rams for 2. 6 hp (scales).

Flux Moth The Alkaline Zone4 scrapPairs

Dark metal drone with two pairs of shimmering foil wings held upright and facing you, like a real moth: large forewings above, rounder hindwings below, cyan eyespots. Flies in pairs that sweep down the field together, the gap between them breathing wider and narrower, joined by a jagged, faintly glowing electric arc (1 damage, at most once a second). Slip through the gap or break the pair: when one dies the arc goes out, but the survivor keeps flying and still hurts on contact (1 damage). Loops back to the top until destroyed. 5 hp each. 1 pair per wave, 2 from sector 26, +1 in elite sectors.

Ooze Sector 21+1 scrap per blobSplits

Green slime blob. Never shoots. Creeps above your row, squashes flat and flashes (the tell), then lunges at you and splats goo on your row. Leaves a slime trail; goo slows you. Each kill splits it into two smaller, faster copies (big → 2 medium → 4 small, 7 in all). 1 per wave from sector 21, 2 from sector 26, +1 in elite sectors from 19.

Capital units that end a zone. Easy bosses guard the early sectors.

Easy 3

Bastion Open Zone: 5, 10+60 scrap

Slab-hulled carrier with three bolted-on turrets. The core is armored until the turrets are shot off; the core fires a five-shot fan.

Warden Open Zone: 5, 10+60 scrap

Giant mech with an arm beam cannon. Laser-sight tell, then an aimed beam every few seconds; flashes half the field red, then sweeps a beam from that edge to the centre. Heavily armored; calls in skirmishers.

Minotaur Open Zone: 5, 10+60 scrap

Oversized Heavy with bull horns, twin shoulder cannons and a black inverted triangle on its forehead. Cannons fire aimed shots left, right, left at a steady beat. Every 5–8 s it stops, shudders and flashes a red lane at you, then rams down it for 3 damage.

Medium 5

The Magpie Silent Hulk event: 10, 15, 20…+40 scrapOnce per run

Scavenger mothership patched together from stolen hulls: black and white hull, a glossy black beak-colored nose cone, rust and olive scrap wings, a teal eye and a tractor claw. Only fought by boarding the Silent Hulk (25% chance, once per run, right before a capital unit from sector 10). Stage 1: three derelict hulks circle in front as shields (one dragged back in every 8 s) and it fires single aimed shots. Tractor beam: a teal lane flashes for 1 s, then for 2 s it pulls you in and steals 5 scrap a second. Stage 2 (half health): its wings tear off and break into 6 chunks that tumble down the lane (1 damage on contact, can't be shot), plus 3-shot spreads and a red beam that sweeps edge to edge (1 damage). 70 hp (scales). Win: +40 scrap, stolen scrap back and the Magpie Claw, then straight into the capital unit. Losing ends the run.

The Caesar Legion Space: 15, 20+60 scrapLegion Space

Golden mech legionary: red iron crest on a T-visor helmet, round shield with red trim, spear. Bulky but rounded. Shoulder cannon over the shield fires 3-shot bursts that mildly track. Closes in, lowers its spear (red zone flashes) and sweeps it for 2 damage. Shudders, flashes a red lane and shield-rams for 3, then resets to the back middle.

The Glacier Frost Reach: 15, 20+60 scrapThe Frost Reach

Ice-bound juggernaut warship: dark hull, bridge tower, side sponsons, prow frost cannon, all encased in a jagged ice shell. Fires slow 5-shard fans; drops freeze fields on you (blue ring flashes, then halves your speed while inside, 4 s); calls in Rime Motes. At half health the shell shatters: faster fans, more fields.

Dry Ice Frost Reach: 15, 20+60 scrapThe Frost Reach

Frosty interceptor: wings fanning back in steps, a fin of ice, always wrapped in mist. Strafes back and forth firing straight rounds that loosely home in. Every 7–9 s mist rolls over your view; it repositions while you are blind, flashes a red lane, then fires a wide beam straight ahead (2 damage).

Onager Legion Space: 15, 20+60 scrapLegion Space

A literal Roman onager scaled up into a flying siege engine: heavy timber frame on glowing hover-wheels, twisted rope torsion skein, A-frame with a padded red crossbar, bronze fittings, neon trim. Stage 1: holds the centre and lobs EMP bombs from its throwing arm (winches back, snaps up against the crossbar) (blue ring marks the landing: 1 damage, speed halved for 1.5 s) while Lancers make their runs; the hull is armored until the throwing arm (separate target) is destroyed. Stage 2: rams like a Lancer (hover, red lane, charge for 3), then returns to the top.

Hard 7

The Void Priest Dark Space: 35+45 scrap

A priestly Void Prophet: pointed head with a glowing eye slit, a wider embroidered stole in neon purple and grey with a matching skirt, and a staff topped by a glowing purple orb. Never dives. Stage 1: drifts across the top firing one wide, loosely homing bolt from the orb (1 damage); every ~5 s the orb flares (the tell) and it fires three wider bolts in quick succession at the left, middle and right thirds of the lane (2 damage each). Stage 2 (half health): drifts faster, and the three bolts start loosely homing once they're halfway down the lane. 150 hp (scales with the sector).

The Void Prophet Rare: 25, 30, 35+45 scrapOnce per run

A larger Void Bot with a pointed head (glowing violet eye slit), a neon purple and grey priest's stole and a staff. Replaces the usual boss 10% of the time at sector 25 and 20% at sector 30, and is half the sector 35 pool; it appears at most once per run. Stage 1: floats across the top firing 3-shot fans from its staff head, and dives like a Void Bot (zips to one side, then the other, then charges straight at you, no homing; 3 damage). Stage 2 (half health): the staff breaks, it fires 5-shot fans from its eye slit, and its dives weakly home. 140 hp (scales with the sector).

Null Choir Dark Space: 35+45 scrap3 parts

Three floating obsidian obelisks shaped like the Washington Monument (tall, slightly tapering square shaft with a pyramid cap), each banded with 1, 2 or 3 violet rings, with a violet rune slit and a crystal at the tip. They hum in turn: the humming one glows, a violet guide line flashes toward you for 1 s, then a beam fires along it (2 damage). Turns come every 1.3 s and overlap, so you weave between lanes. Each also lobs a slow aimed orb every 3–4 s. Silence them low to high (1 ring, then 2, then 3); kill one out of order and the rest sing 35% faster (stacks). 55 hp each (scales); all three must fall.

Geode Red Crystal: boss sectors+60 scrap

Huge sard-red asteroid bristling with crystal spikes around a glowing core. Spins, firing 16-shard rings with a 3-shard gap to slip through; at half health rings come faster with an aimed 3-shot from the core between them. Calls in Splinters.

Refraction Red Crystal: boss sectors+60 scrap

Crystalline warship with a prism on its back and swept crystal wings. Strafes firing 3-shard spreads; every 5 s charges the prism and splits its beam: red guide lines fan out (3, or 5 at half health) for 1.1 s, then lasers fire along them (2 damage).

The Crucible Alkaline: boss sectors+60 scrap

Smelting-furnace warship of riveted wrought iron with a wide span of stepped iron wings: near-black hull with iron bands and scrollwork, a furnace house with a bright flickering forge glow at its mouth (flaring brighter once the door blows off), twin chimneys, side pouring spouts, ram prow. Both stages: lobs slow slag blobs at you. Pours molten metal into a lane: an orange strip glows (tell), molten metal floods it (2 damage), then it cools into a long iron wall down the lane for 5 s that funnels you (touching it costs 1 and shoves you out). At half health the furnace door blows off: two lanes at once, one each side of you (a corridor), and 5-blob slag fans.

Mercury Alkaline: boss sectors+60 scrap

Slender humanoid mech of liquid metal: every slice of its body is offset along a sine wave, so the limbs and torso ripple like a falling stream; pinched waist, off-centre head with a cyan visor, puddling feet, drooping arms that run down into drips. It sways gently as it moves. Splits into two flickering copies, left and right; only the real one takes damage and it glows brighter. Hit the copy and it bursts violently (8 shards outward), then Mercury re-splits 3 s later and may swap sides. Both copies fire simple 3-shot spreads. Big attack: one copy flashes a red quarter of the field (tell), then lashes a liquid-metal whip across that quarter (2 damage).

Final 1

Scion of the Void Gods Dark Space: 40Very hardWins the run

A slender Minotaur in purple, black and dark blue: T-visor face, a double cannon on each shoulder, long arms holding a gnarled black staff outlined in pale grey, and a Void Priest stole. 260 hp (scales), and a 1 s shudder (can't be hurt) at each stage change. Stage 1: alternating double shots from its cannons (every 0.5 s), a homing beam bolt from its staff every 3 s, and Minotaur-style charges: it glows red and flashes a red lane for 1.2 s, holding fire, then rams for 3. Stage 2 (60%): the stole tears away and it charges more often, for 4. Stage 3 (30%): no more charges; it fires 30% faster and throws one Void Bomb at a time (every ~5 s) into the left, middle or right third of the lane (never your third twice in a row): a weak pull toward it for 3 s, then a burst of colored voxels (2 damage to anything in that third). Beating it ends the run with a Victory screen and unlocks Void God, Nox (see 08 Post Game).

Zones 6

The Open Zone 5 sectorsSectors 1–10

The starting area: two bosses, at sectors 5 and 10, picked at random from Bastion, Warden and Minotaur. Sectors 1–4 are combat, sector 5 is a boss (then 6–9 and 10). Take 3 hits or fewer across the four combat sectors and a Bonus Stage opens as sector 4.5. Plain starfield, no hazards. Its name shows on each sector banner.

Legion Space Branch: sectors 11–20Backdrop builtBosses: The Caesar or Onager

Cyberpunk meets the Roman Legion. Backdrop (built): stars over black space with sandy dust and haze; floating sandstone ruins with neon cyan and pink trim drift past on both sides: colonnades, arches, cyber towers with gold domes and, now and then, a Parthenon. Chosen at the branch after sector 10 (the only choice for now; two more zones to come). Bosses at 15 and 20 are picked from The Caesar and Onager. After sector 20 the branch repeats. Occasional obstacles drift through the field for the player to avoid (ideas: legion standard pylons, shield-wall barriers, debris from a cyber-colosseum). Obstacle built: Fallen Column, a lane glows red for 1 s, then a broken marble column tumbles down it every 6–10 s (1 damage on touch; 6 hp, shoot it apart for 2 scrap). Unit: Praetorian, 2 per wave. Bosses: The Caesar or Onager. Music: Iron Eagles (sectors), Siege of Steel (boss).

The Frost Reach Branch: sectors 11–20BuiltBosses: The Glacier or Dry Ice

Icy space wreckage. Backdrop: ice floes, icebergs and frozen warship hulks drift past below the field, snow dust blows sideways, an aurora ripples far ahead; space turns a deep blue-black; translucent white and pale blue frost veils and glassy ice crystals drift by. Bosses: The Glacier or Dry Ice. Obstacle: Ice Shard, a red lane flashes at an angle from a side edge, then a shard slides across it (1 damage; 4 hp, 2 scrap). Unit: Ice Golem, 2 per wave (Rime Motes only come with The Glacier). Music: Whiteout Drift (sectors), Glacier Heart (boss).

Red Crystal Asteroid Belt HardBranch from sector 21Bosses: Geode or Refraction

A cave of floating crystals: red and light red crystal clusters on sard-red rock line the floor, walls and ceiling around the field; pale red haze and distant sard asteroids behind; deep sard-red space. Offered at the branch after sector 20 and later. Hard: wave units have +25% health. Unit: Splinter, 3 per wave. Music: Scarlet Facets (sectors), Heart of the Geode (boss).

The Alkaline Zone HardBranch from sector 21Bosses: The Crucible or Mercury

Metallic space based on alkali metals: polished ingots, slabs and lumps of soft grey metal turn and catch the light as they drift past, white glints wink across them, silver dust, and shimmering sheets of faded grey light over a grey sky. Hard: wave units +25% health. Calcium Build-Up: long white clumps grown out of a dark metal mass beyond the edge stretch into the lanes (one side, or both 30% of the time with a gap left), scrolling with the terrain to funnel you (1 damage on contact). 30% of them shed a chunk that floats down the lane (1 damage; 4 hp, 2 scrap). Shimmer: every 10–16 s a metallic heat-shimmer blurs the view for about 2 s. Music: Ferrous Line (sectors), Caustic Engine (boss). Unit: Flux Moth pairs. Bosses: The Crucible or Mercury.

Dark Space Final zoneBranch from sector 31In progress

The wide open dark between the stars: the last stretch of the campaign, ending with the final boss at sector 40. Offered at the branch after sector 30. Backdrop: pitch-black space with only the odd star (1 in 30 of the usual), the lane running over a dim, deep nebula of purple, pink and blue drifting far below. Hazard: Void Orb, a pitch-black sphere with only a faint white-yellow halo and a faint glowing rim around its edge, hard to see (up close it reads faintly as a 3D ball); drifts down the lane every 3.6–5.8 s (5–7.2 s in boss sectors), costs 2 armor on contact, can't be destroyed. Unit: Void Bot, 2 per wave (3 from sector 36, +1 in elites). Music: Into the Dark (sectors) and The Last Light (boss), both opening on a quiet, suspenseful drum intro before the full band. Bosses at 35: Null Choir, The Void Priest or The Void Prophet (if it hasn't appeared yet this run). Final boss at 40: Scion of the Void Gods; beating it wins the run.

Sounds 5

Scion's death roar Final boss diesBuilt

A metallic roar as the Scion shudders and fades: distorted, detuned sawtooths falling 110 → 38 Hz through a sweeping bandpass with a rattling tremolo, over ringing inharmonic metal partials.

Signal Static Opening reelBuilt

Soft ambient static behind the opening reel: looped white noise through a drifting bandpass (1.2–2.8 kHz) and a lowpass, with slow random swells so it fades in and out like a weak transmission. Fades away when the reel ends or is skipped. Follows the Music volume.

Power-up boop On pickup

A soft rising sine "boop" (620 → 930 Hz, under 0.2 s) when you fly over a power-up.

Blaster Every other volley

A low, short filtered sawtooth zap (190 → 70 Hz) on every second volley you fire, so high fire rates stay rhythmic instead of noisy.

Low armor warning Armor ≤ 25%Once a second

A soft, low triangle-wave blip (330 Hz) that repeats while armor is in the red.
